About the Project

This platform demonstrates a Hadoop-based air quality analytics workflow: preprocessing, HDFS storage, MapReduce job execution, and API-driven dashboard visualization.

The core analytics remain powered by Hadoop outputs, while the frontend consumes those results for charts and KPI summaries.
